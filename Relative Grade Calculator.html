How it works: Specifies the top percentile (e.g., top 5%) who receive S grade. Enter percentile value (1-50).

Marks below this get U grade

Grade Scale Reference

S Top percentile (Excellent)
A Group 1 below S (Very Good)
B Group 2 below S (Good)
C Group 3 below S (Average)
D Group 4 below S (Below Avg)
E Group 5 below S (Pass)
U Below threshold (Fail)
Algorithm: Student marks are ranked by percentile. Top percentile gets S. Remaining marks (below S to fail threshold) are divided equally into 5 groups (A→E). Below threshold = U.
